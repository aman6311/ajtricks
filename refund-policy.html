Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

1. Refunds

We offer refunds for damaged or incorrect items. Please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order to request a refund.

2. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, orders cannot be cancelled.

3. Processing Time

Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
