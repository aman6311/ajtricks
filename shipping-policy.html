Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

1. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated during checkout based on weight, dimensions and destination of the items in the order.

2. Delivery Timeline

Orders are usually processed within 1-2 business days. Delivery times vary by location, typically taking 3-7 business days.

3. Tracking

You will receive a tracking link via email/SMS once your order has shipped.
